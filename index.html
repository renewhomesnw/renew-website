About

Built for the
long term

Renew Homes Northwest is a Seattle-based residential infill developer focused on teardown-rebuild projects in NR2, NR3, and NC-zoned neighborhoods.

We combine hands-on construction experience with a data-driven acquisition approach — identifying underutilized lots and delivering homes that fit the neighborhood while raising the bar for quality and livability.

Every project is designed to endure: high-quality materials, thoughtful layouts, and finishes chosen to age gracefully in Seattle's climate.
