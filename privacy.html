Overview

Renew Homes Northwest LLC ("Renew", "we", "us") operates renew.design and related services. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and phone number when you contact us through our website or sign up for our services. We also collect standard website usage data including pages visited, browser type, and referring URLs.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide our services, send communications you've requested, and improve our website. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Communications (Voz.ai)

If you use the Voz.ai workforce check-in platform, your phone number is used solely to send shift dispatch notifications and recap summaries as part of your employment arrangement with a Voz.ai customer organization. No marketing messages are sent. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at team@renew.design.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
